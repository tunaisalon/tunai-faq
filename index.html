E-Invoice & LHDN compliance
Malaysia-built
Yes — and not just partially. Tunai integrates directly with the Malaysian government's LHDN e-Invoice API, which means your invoices are submitted to MyInvois automatically the moment a sale is completed. No third-party middleware, no extra subscriptions, no manual export-import.
Fully native: The LHDN integration is built inside Tunai, not bolted on through another service. One less tool, one less monthly fee, one less thing to break.
This matters as Malaysia's e-invoicing mandate progressively covers more businesses through 2025–2026.
Most salon systems in Malaysia either don't support e-Invoice at all, or rely on a third-party service (such as JomeInvoice) to submit on your behalf — which adds cost and an extra point of failure.

Tunai built its LHDN integration directly using the government API. Complete a sale → invoice generated → submitted to MyInvois. Automatic. No extra steps, no extra tools, no extra bill.
